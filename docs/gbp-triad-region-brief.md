## Editorial Rules

- One appliance/service, city, and normally one ZIP per post. Never dump the full ZIP list into a post.
- Vary the problem and practical information; do not publish near-identical city-swap copies.
- Use service availability language, never invented local jobs or local branch claims.
- Target roughly 300-600 characters for readability; length and ZIP frequency are not ranking rules.
- Use Book with the supplied direct HCP URL. Verify the live booking flow accepts the expanded address before scheduling. No unverified city landing-page URLs.
- Photographs illustrate the appliance/service; their job locations and outcomes are unconfirmed.
- Approved branding: existing `public/gbp-posts/dapl-logo-sticker.png`. Do not remove internal white/silver details or add large banners.
- Avoid private family photos, identifiable customers without permission, and unsuitable work-position images. Do not treat visually repeated shots as separate jobs.
- Prepared, scheduled, and published are separate statuses. This batch starts as Draft, not Published.

## SEO Expectations And Profile Coverage

Google identifies relevance, distance, and prominence/popularity as its main local-ranking factors. It does not document a ranking bonus from two daily posts or inserting ZIP codes. Posts can communicate service and support customer decisions; measure bookings and leads rather than assuming rank improvements.

Google permits up to 20 service-area entries, using cities, postal codes or other supported areas. The profile's overall service area should generally stay within about two hours' drive of its base. The full 33-ZIP inventory plus existing coverage therefore should not be entered as 33 separate GBP service areas. Select supported city/area entries reflecting actual operations and check the overall drive-time boundary. No fictitious office or second profile.

For broader regional growth, review accurate profile categories/services, address eligibility and coverage, actual customer reviews without incentives, helpful regional website content based on real operations, and legitimate local links. Website regional pages need their own implementation task and should have useful unique content rather than ZIP-swapped duplicates.

Review the campaign after 14-28 days: published/rejected status, HCP bookings by service ZIP where available, qualified calls, and comparable rank tracking from fixed locations. These are observations, not proof that posts caused ranking changes.
