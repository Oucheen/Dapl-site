## Posting Strategy

- Publish two GBP posts per week.
- Space posts out by 3-4 days when scheduling allows.
- Use one main service keyword per post.
- Use one city or one nearby city pair per post.
- Do not mention every service area in every post.
- Keep each week's two posts meaningfully different.
- Good weekly pairs:
  - residential laundry + kitchen appliance
  - Charlotte or Mint Hill + nearby suburb
  - residential appliance + commercial appliance
  - high-intent service page + local service-area page
- Keep writing natural, specific, and useful.
- Mention the city or area early in the post when possible.
- Tie the post to a real appliance problem a customer would recognize.
- Rotate residential and commercial jobs so the GBP profile looks active and broad.
- Use real DAPL field photos whenever available.

## Photo Strategy

- Use the real job photo as the main image.
- Add only the DAPL company logo as a small clean overlay or corner badge.
- Do not add large text banners.
- Do not add service names, city names, phone numbers, fake badges, prices, ratings, coupons, or warranty claims on the image unless explicitly requested.
- Keep the photo authentic and recognizable.
- Avoid customer faces, exact addresses, paperwork, invoices, license plates, or private information.
- If a photo is too messy or unclear, use it only if it still clearly shows the appliance, component, or repair context.

## Tone

- Clear
- Local
- Practical
- Professional
- Not overhyped
- No fake urgency
- No unsupported promises

## Avoid

- `Guaranteed`
- `Certified` unless verified for the exact claim
- `Best in Charlotte`
- `Cheapest`
- `24/7` unless truly offered
- `Same-day` unless phrased as `when scheduling allows`
- Repeating the same sentence structure every week
- Keyword stuffing
- Mentioning all cities in one post
- Reusing recently published photos too soon

## Good Wording

- `when scheduling allows`
- `nearby Charlotte-area homes`
- `local appliance repair visit`
- `route availability`
- `diagnostics and repair`
- `practical repair guidance`
- `homeowners in Mint Hill`
- `businesses around Fort Mill and Rock Hill`
