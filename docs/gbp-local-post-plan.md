## Publishing Rules

- Publish two posts per week.
- Space posts out by 3-4 days when scheduling allows.
- Pair each week so the two posts are not too similar.
- Do not mention all service areas in every post.
- Use one primary service keyword per post.
- Use one city or one city pair per post.
- Keep most descriptions between 300 and 600 characters.
- Use real field photos; add only a small DAPL logo overlay unless the user explicitly asks for text on the image.
- Rotate residential and commercial posts so the profile does not look repetitive.
- Prefer service-page links for appliance-specific posts.
- Prefer city-page links for broad local posts.
- Use `Call now` only when the post feels urgent or commercial.
