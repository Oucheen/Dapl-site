## Rules For Strong GBP Posts

- Use one clear service keyword per post.
- Mention one or two nearby cities, not every city at once.
- Use real job photos whenever possible.
- Keep description under 600 characters when possible.
- Choose `Book` for normal repair posts.
- Choose `Call now` for urgent commercial refrigeration or same-day style posts.
- Link to the closest matching service page first; use city pages when the post is more area-focused than appliance-focused.
